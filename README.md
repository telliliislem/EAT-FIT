# 🥗 EAT & FIT

> **EAT & FIT** is an all-in-one health and fitness platform built with **Django** that connects users with professional fitness coaches, certified nutritionists, and healthy food businesses. The platform provides personalized fitness programs, nutrition plans, appointment booking, an online marketplace for healthy products, secure online payments, and an AI-powered assistant to help users achieve a healthier lifestyle.

---

## 📖 About the Project

EAT & FIT is designed to bring the entire health and wellness ecosystem into a single platform.

Whether users want to:
- Find a personal coach,
- Book a nutrition consultation,
- Buy healthy meals or protein supplements,
- Discover healthy recipes,
- Follow personalized workout programs,
- Or simply ask questions about nutrition and fitness,

they can do everything from one application.

The platform is developed using **Django** for both the backend and frontend, following the **Model-View-Template (MVT)** architecture.
